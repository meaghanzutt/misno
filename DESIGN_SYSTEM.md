# MISNÖ Design System

## Palette
- Background: `#FFFFFF`
- Surface: `#FAFAFA`
- Text: `#111111`
- Muted: `#6B7280`
- Border: `#E5E7EB`
- Accent: `#2F5D50`

## Typography
Inter with weights 400, 500, 600, 700.

## Geometry
Card radius: 16px. Controls: 12px. One subtle card shadow: `0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.05)`.

## Interaction
One primary action per screen. Primary buttons are black and hover forest green. Secondary buttons are white with a gray border. Motion is limited to short fades, color changes, and small shifts.
