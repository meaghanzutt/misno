# PR #6 — Discover

## Goal

Help members find an experience, neighborhood, circle, or person that feels worth showing up for.

## Included

- Search across all Discover content.
- Four accessible content tabs: Experiences, Neighborhoods, Circles, and People.
- Experience category filters.
- Six realistic local experience examples.
- Neighborhood, Circle, and Person card components.
- Responsive layouts and keyboard focus states.
- Clear empty-search state.
- No stock photography.

## QA

- Open `/app/discover`.
- Search for `coffee`, `San Diego`, and `culture`.
- Switch through all four tabs.
- Test every category chip.
- Confirm the layout at mobile, tablet, and desktop widths.
- Confirm keyboard focus is visible.
