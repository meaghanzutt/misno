# Experience Feature Specification

## Problem
Community activity becomes confusing when rooms, events, and groups float independently.

## User story
As a member, I want one clear Experience page so I can understand what is happening, who is involved, and what I can join next.

## Simplest version
Overview, Members, Rooms, Events, and Passport activity.

## Acceptance criteria
- An Experience may be local or global.
- Rooms and Events belong to an Experience.
- Joining creates a Passport entry.
- Hosts can manage content from Workspace.

## Out of scope for Sprint 1
Payments, ticketing, analytics, and production persistence.
