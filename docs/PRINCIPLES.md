# MISNÖ Principles

1. **Build for belonging.** We optimize for participation and relationships, not addictive engagement.
2. **Experiences first.** Circles, Rooms, Events, Members, and Resources support an Experience.
3. **Calm beats clever.** White space, predictable layouts, and one primary action per screen.
4. **Membership represents contribution.** Higher tiers unlock responsibility and creation tools.
5. **Earn complexity.** Advanced capabilities appear only when members are ready.
6. **Local matters, but global is allowed.** Experiences may belong to a Neighborhood or exist online.
