# MISNÖ Product Decisions

## Decision 001: Experiences are the center of the product
Experiences organize people, rooms, events, discussions, and Passport activity.

## Decision 002: Workspace is earned
Workspace appears for Contributors, Partners, and Executive Partners because membership progression represents responsibility.

## Decision 003: No AI community assistant in Version 1
MISNÖ prioritizes human connection and practical host resources.

## Decision 004: The Ö is the membership mark
Every person receives an Ö beside their display name. Its treatment reflects membership level.

## Decision 005: White-first visual design
The interface uses white, black, gray, and restrained forest green to remain calm and easy to understand.
