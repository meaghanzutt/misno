# MISNÖ Product Bible

## Vision
Help people discover meaningful experiences and build genuine communities.

## Core product model
An **Experience** is the central object. It may be local or global and can contain Circles, Rooms, Events, Members, Gallery items, Discussions, and Resources.

## Product pillars
- **Discover:** Find experiences, neighborhoods, circles, and people.
- **Participate:** Join conversations, rooms, events, and experiences.
- **Contribute:** Use Workspace to create and lead.

## Signature concepts
- **Passport:** A chronological record of meaningful participation, not points or XP.
- **Ö mark:** A membership mark displayed beside each member name.
- **Workspace / NÖMAD Studio:** Creation tools for Contributors and above.

## MVP navigation
Public: Home, Memberships, About, Contact.
Community: Home, Discover, Messages, Passport, Profile.
Workspace: Overview, Experiences, Rooms, Events, Resources.
Admin: Dashboard, Members, Reports, Settings.
