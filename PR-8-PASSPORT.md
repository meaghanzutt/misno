# PR #8 — Passport

## Goal
Give every member a calm, meaningful record of participation without points, streaks, or competitive gamification.

## Included
- Story-first Passport header and summary.
- Filters for attended, hosted, upcoming, and community moments.
- Month-grouped timeline entries.
- Joined Experiences pulled from the existing browser demo state.
- Direct links back to Experience details.
- Responsive and keyboard-accessible controls.
- Clear empty states.

## Test
1. Open `/app/passport`.
2. Switch through every filter.
3. Join an Experience from Discover, then return to Passport.
4. Verify the joined Experience appears under “Next up.”
5. Open a linked Passport entry and confirm it returns to the correct Experience.
